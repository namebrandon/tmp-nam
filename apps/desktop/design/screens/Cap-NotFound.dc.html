Connect the Tone Master Pro

The unit isn't connected. Captures are read from the unit, so this page needs it.

  1. Insert the NAM SD card and power the unit on.
  2. Wait for the preset screen.
  3. Plug the unit's USB-C cable into this Mac.

It's detected automatically. Nothing to click here.

No NAM SD card yet? The unit has to boot from a NAM card for this app to see it. You can make one with a USB card reader.
