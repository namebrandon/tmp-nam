TMP NAM · Design system v1

Foundations

A native-feeling Mac tool, dark appearance only: the window stays dark whatever the system setting. Neutral surfaces, one blue for actions and selection, three status colours that only ever mean status. System font, 13 px body, the same metrics as AppKit controls.

Rules the screens follow Blue = something you can do or have selected. Never decoration. Green, amber, red = unit/file/operation status. Always paired with text. Icons only where they name a place or a state. No illustrations. Labels say what happens: "Send 3 Captures", not "Continue".
